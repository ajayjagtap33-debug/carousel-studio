## The look: three layers on one page

The old version was hand-drawn text plus sticky notes, so every slide looked the same. This one keeps the hand-drawn
feel but gives each slide **its own picture**, built from three layers:

| layer | what it is | where it comes from | job on the slide |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sketch** (raw) | hand-drawn headline, our cast (`person.*`, `bot.*`), doodles, arrows, scribbles, circles drawn over things | the Excalidraw theme as now: Virgil, off-white paper, one accent (orange, warm) | the voice: it feels like someone drew it for you |
| **Clean** (crisp) | real UI rebuilt clean: an iMessage thread, an inbox, a ChatGPT box, a timer | the studio scenes, rendered to PNG and placed as an image | the "AI world", tidy and a bit cold on purpose |
| **Cutout** (real) | real objects with the background removed, white sticker edge, soft shadow, tilted 2–6° | photos (yours preferred, see list), cut out by a script | the "human world": paper, ink, mugs, voices, faces |

The contrast is the point: **clean = the AI shortcut, cutout + sketch = the human way.** Rules that keep it from getting messy:
- One hero picture per slide. At most one cutout, one clean piece, and the cast.
- Hand-drawn marks always sit **on top** of clean and cutout pieces (an arrow, a circle, a strike, a scribble). That's what makes them feel like one page.
- Clean pieces are a little greyed or struck when they stand for the shortcut. Cutouts are full colour.
- Type stays the carousel system: headline max 2 lines with body, body 1 line, visual ≥520px, text ≥36px.
- No text inside any photo or generated image. Words are always our type.

4. The giant flat phone for slides 1 and 10 is one new clean piece: a phone in perspective, drawn once.
