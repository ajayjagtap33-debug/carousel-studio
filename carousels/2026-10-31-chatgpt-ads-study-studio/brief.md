# Brief

**Takeaway (required):** ChatGPT ads are early and thin, they don't work like Google Ads, and brands that don't advertise can still have rivals shown on their name.

**Source material (required):** "We studied 7,497 ChatGPT Ads: What they tell us about the new ad layer", Piyush Lathiya, Track My Visibility, 2026-09-30.
https://trackmyvisibility.com/blogs/research/chatgpt-ads-analysis/

**Numbers & claims allowed** (all from the study above):
- 7,497 ad observations · 1,957 distinct questions · 1,346 distinct advertisers
- 63 questions named a specific company; that company had 0 of the 180 ads (they came from 94 other companies)
- 65.1% of questions (1,274 of 1,957) showed ads from exactly one advertiser
- 777 of 1,346 advertisers (57.7%) appeared on only one question
- "The room is close to empty." (study's own words)
- 86.2% of questions naming a non-US market returned no advertiser from that market (50 of 58: India, Australia, NZ, UAE)
- Informational questions: 3.92 ads each (388 q, 1,520 ads). Transactional: 3.43 (380 q, 1,305 ads)
- utm_term on 806 links; only 43 (5.3%) share a single word with the question. "No search term coming back to you."
- All 7,497 links carry OpenAI's `oppref` token; none carry a Google click ID (gclid)
- 42.1% of links (3,154) carry no utm_source
- 26.4% of advertisers (355) only ever linked their homepage
- 71 advertisers built landing pages specifically for this channel
- Recommendations: check tracking before scaling; treat paid and organic AI visibility as separate
