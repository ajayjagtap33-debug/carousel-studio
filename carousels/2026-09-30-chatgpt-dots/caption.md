OpenAI just launched dots in ChatGPT. Here's what I'd actually hand to mine.

The difference is simple:
ChatGPT waits for you.
Your dot keeps working when you're gone.

So I stopped thinking about prompts.
I started thinking about jobs. Things I check again and again, and would happily give to someone else.

8 of them:

👉 Watch a price for me (ping me only when the iPhone drops below ₹60,000)
👉 Turn my inbox into a to-do list every evening (replies I owe, deadlines buried in threads, newsletters skipped)
👉 Brief me before every meeting (5 minutes, from my calendar, emails and docs)
👉 Hunt jobs and research each company before it reaches me
👉 Research one big question in the background ("Is AI search cutting website traffic?")
👉 Coach me one lesson a day and remember where I left off
👉 Catch the customer requests that keep coming back
👉 Find opportunities I'd miss (grants, speaking slots, calls for contributors)

The last one is my favourite.
AI could already search the web. The new part is that it keeps looking, without me asking again.

You don't have to know what to ask AI anymore.
You can give it a responsibility.

To be fair, this is my list on day 2 of the launch. I haven't tested all of these yet.

Which one would you hand off first? Or what job would you give yours?
