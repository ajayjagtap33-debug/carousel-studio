# Brief

**Takeaway (required):** When someone asks ChatGPT about your brand, the ads can all come from other companies. Worth checking what shows up for yours.

**Source material (required):** Track My Visibility study "We studied 7,497 ChatGPT Ads" (Piyush Lathiya, 2026-09-30), section "In all 63 questions that named a specific company, the ads came from other companies".
https://trackmyvisibility.com/blogs/research/chatgpt-ads-analysis/

**Numbers & claims allowed** (quoted from that section, checked 2026-10-05):
- "Appsumo vs other stores for sales software deals" returned 27 ads, none of them AppSumo.
- "Symphony Limited vs other brands for industrial cooling systems" returned 31 ads, none of them Symphony.
- 63 questions named a real company, across 50 distinct companies.
- They returned 180 ads from 94 advertisers. The named company had 0 of the 180, on 0 of the 63 questions.
- Question shapes were like "X vs other agencies" and "alternatives to X" (honest caveat: these questions invite comparison).
