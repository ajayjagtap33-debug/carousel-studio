Someone asked ChatGPT: "Appsumo vs other stores for sales software deals"

27 ads showed up.

Not one of them was AppSumo. 🧐

And it wasn't a one-off.

We studied 7,497 ChatGPT ads. 63 questions named a real company.
Those questions returned 180 ads from 94 advertisers.

The company in the question had 0 of them.

To be fair, these were "X vs others" and "alternatives to X" type questions.
So seeing competitors there is expected.

The part that surprised me is that the brand itself was never in the list.
Not even once.

So if someone asks ChatGPT about your brand today, the only ads they see can belong to someone else.

Has anyone here actually checked what shows up when people ask ChatGPT about your brand?
