We studied 7,497 ChatGPT ads.

Across 1,957 questions and 1,346 advertisers.

Some of it surprised us. Some of it was just weird.

Here are the 6 findings:

👉 Ask about a brand, get someone else's ad.
In 63 questions that named a company, that company had 0 of the 180 ads.

👉 The room is close to empty.
65.1% of questions showed ads from just one advertiser.

👉 Local questions don't get local ads.
50 of 58 questions about India, Australia, NZ or the UAE had no advertiser from that market.

👉 How-to questions get ads too.
3.92 ads per question, vs 3.43 for "where do I buy" questions.

👉 Your usual tracking won't see it.
Every link carries OpenAI's oppref. None carry a gclid.
And only 5.3% of utm_terms share even one word with the question.

👉 Few built a page for ChatGPT.
71 advertisers did. 26.4% only ever linked their homepage.

To be fair, these are patterns we saw in our data.
They don't tell you how often ads show up, or what people do after they see one.

But if you don't run ads, finding #1 should bother you a little.
