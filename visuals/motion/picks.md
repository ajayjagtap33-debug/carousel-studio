# Motion picks: what to reuse for YouTube videos and reels

Chosen from the 236 in [README.md](README.md) by their title, site category, length, pace and poster frame. **Not yet watched end to end**: open `local/videos/<id>.mp4` before building from one, and treat the notes as a pointer to what to look for. Per-beat pieces are also in `visuals/catalog.md` (source key PM) under their meaning. Borrow the mechanism and timing, never the piece.

Our look still wins: Instrument Serif + italic accent, sentence case, no all-caps kickers, both themes (`tmv`, `ajay`). Most pieces here use other fonts and dark HUD labels; take the motion, not the styling.
