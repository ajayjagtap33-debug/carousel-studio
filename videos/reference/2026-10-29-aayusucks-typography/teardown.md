# Teardown: aayusucks, "Typography Without Bullsh*t (Masterclass)"

Source: youtube.com/watch?v=BWRdNeNkph4 — 17:19, published 2025-12-16. Ajay's reference (2026-10-29) for the
**text we put over him when he's full frame** in studio mode. Method: 1080p download, contact sheets every 5s
(`frames/s01–07.png`), 2fps sheet of the cold open (`frames/open.png`), full frames `frames/f*.png`.
Transcript in `transcript.txt`. Frames are private reference.

## Text over the face shots

- **Most face shots carry no text at all.** Of ~90 face frames on the sheets, only the cold open (0:00–0:14) has words
  on him. The talking is clean; the type happens elsewhere.
- **No wash, no panel, no box.** White words straight on the footage (a warm, mid-bright wall), at most a faint glyph
  shadow. Nothing darkens the picture, so nothing creeps onto the face.
- **Word by word, on the voice.** "Look i am going to tell you something" builds one word per spoken word along
  one line at chest height, crossing in front of his hand — never across the eyes/mouth.
- **Phrase split around the head**: "Think like" left of the face, "an artist" right of it, at eye level.
- **Scattered grid** (0:10): every word of the sentence pinned to a loose 6×3 grid around him, small (~22px at 1080),
  sans with the stressed words (*training*, *decide*, *typography*) in a script italic. Looks good, barely readable;
  works only because it's 3s of the hook.
- Type: a neo-grotesk (Helvetica Neue / Neue Haas look), regular-to-medium at 60–70px, normal tracking, sentence case.
  No all-caps kickers anywhere, no counters, no labels.

## Full-screen type cards (where the typography really lives)

- Hard cut from his face to an off-white/light-grey page with **one huge word or line**: bold grotesk, black, tracked
  tight ("Headline", "Tracking", "HELLO", "48px HEADLINE / 58px LINE HEIGHT"), 2–4s, then back to him.
- **Accent = one thing per card**: a highlighter block behind the line (pink, yellow strip), a thick coloured underline,
  or one word swapped to an italic/script face ("is *typography*", "*faster*"). Never two accents.
- **Motion**: words slide in with heavy horizontal motion blur, or rack-focus from blurred to sharp; letters sometimes
  oversized and cropped by the frame edge ("UIS V", "art").
- Real objects (phone, book, statue) sit under or behind the word, never boxed with a caption.

## What his talk says that applies to us

- Large x-height sans reads best on phones; thin "Didone" serifs are for big headers only (strokes vanish small).
- Hierarchy by size jump (≈3×), at most 3 weights, opacity steps 100 / 85 / 60 instead of more fonts.
- Headlines tracked tight (−20 to −40); only all-caps gets added tracking. Squint test: 3 levels must survive.
