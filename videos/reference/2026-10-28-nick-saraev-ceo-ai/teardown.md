# Teardown: Nick Saraev, "How to Use AI Like A Top 0.1% CEO"

Source: youtube.com/watch?v=He-NyJjzWM0 — 7:00, published 2026-10-14. Reference for **studio mode**
(`.claude/skills/broll-video/studio.md`): his face composited into the layout, the output is the finished video.
Method: 1080p download, contact sheets every 3s (`frames/s01–07.png`), 0.1s sequences across transitions
(`frames/t*.png`, named by start second), ffmpeg scene detection for the full-frame cuts. Frames are private reference.

## What defines it

- **Cold open on the face**: 1.3s full frame, hard cut to the board. The first frame is always him.
- **Small face tile**: rounded portrait card ≈190×300 at 1920, right edge, mid-height, soft shadow. The board gets ~90%.
- **Full-frame breathers** every ~40s, 2–5s each, hard cuts on sentence ends: 0:34, 1:23, 1:35, 2:14, 2:22, 3:56,
  4:47, 4:56, 5:37, 5:44, 6:03, 6:21, 6:39, 6:54→end.
- **Shown, not captioned**: scale for trying vs not trying, funnel for a bottleneck, isometric room that furnishes
  itself, a dollar figure counting up then logos landing one by one, real articles with the claim underlined,
  a meeting grid turning into a to-do list, a typed prompt in a chat window.
- **Outline as spine**: "Seven simple upgrades" card returns between sections, ticking the next item (future ones blurred).
- **Motion**: every element blur → sharp with a short fade (~0.2s) on its word; section words arrive in a
  horizontal motion-blur smear; logos stagger ~0.1s apart.
- **Type/colour**: condensed serif display (Instrument Serif look) with italic accent words, small Inter UI text,
  off-white page inside a beige border, one dark-green accent, near-black dark cards.
