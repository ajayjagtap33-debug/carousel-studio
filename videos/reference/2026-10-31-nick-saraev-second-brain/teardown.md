## What's different from his other videos

- **A dark board**: near-black green (#0f140f-ish) with a faint vignette, dark cards with a hairline border, one
  lime-green accent (#A7C77A-ish) for the active thing. Display serif with an italic accent word ("The *nuanced*
  reason", "Recency *weighting*", "Still a *problem*"). Our `ajay` dark card (#111311 + #A7BD82) is close already.
- **Systems, not metaphors.** It's a technical video, so most beats are small diagrams built from the same kit:
  an icon tile + a short label, joined by thin arrows. They build node by node on the words.
- **Face tile bottom-right** for most of it; full frame only at the open, two breathers and the end.

## What we take

- The **pipeline that builds node by node** with labels on the arrows: our `loop` has panels and arrows but no
  labels on the arrows and no branching. → `pipeline`.
- **A container filling up** (context window) → `context`. Works for "your calendar is full", "the inbox", "one page".
- **Sources wiring into one box** → `sources`. Fits every TMV / AEO "we pull from X, Y, Z" line.
- **Acronym spelled out** → `acronym`. AEO, GEO, RAG, SEO come up constantly.
- **Dated cards with recency bars** → `recency`. **Month folders with a look-back arc** → `months`.
- **People converging to one card** → `converge`. **Score tiles** → `scores`.
- Not taken: the status-light rows. It's a list with lights on it, which is the caption-in-a-box we don't do.
