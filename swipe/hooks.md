# Hook library

Patterns, not phrases. Never reuse a reference's wording; reuse the mechanism.
Source key: `okaashish/NN.png` = swipe/others/okaashish/. All 20 refs appear to be one
creator (same avatar + blob character), so treat this as one style, not "what works on
Instagram". Add other creators and your own carousels (with numbers) before trusting it.

Only one ref has numbers: "8 AI tools I stopped paying for" — 4,261 likes, 71 comments
(Instagram, Aug 2026; follower count unknown).

## Headline patterns (cover slide)

| # | Pattern | Mechanism | Refs |
|---|---|---|---|

| H2 | **Subtraction / money** ("I stopped paying for X", "projects that made money") | Loss-aversion + saving money; implies a swap is coming | 02, 18, 19 |

## Cover mechanics (repeat across nearly all refs)

- **Two-line structure:** big headline + small parenthetical/qualifier line underneath
  ("(which level are you at?)", "(the non-technical way)", "(my personal favourites)").
  The second line either lowers the barrier or invites self-identification.
- **One colour-emphasis word** in the headline (UNCENSORED, CLAUDE, 14, AI Slop) or a
  hand-drawn underline under the qualifier. Accent colour often = the subject's brand
  colour (Claude posts use Claude orange).
- **≤ 15 words on the cover.** Headline fills the top ~40%, illustration the bottom.
- **The illustration acts out the hook literally**: scissors cutting strings = stopped
  paying; staircase = levels; doors = types; box opening = uncensored; puddle = slop.
- **Narrator + viewer duo:** a creator avatar explains/acts; a blank character reacts
  (hand on chin, "?") and stands in for the reader's curiosity.
- **Swipe cue** on some covers ("These 5 surprised me →", "MY FREE AI STACK →").

## Inner-slide mechanics (refs 05, 08, 10, 11, 15, 19)

- **Numbered header** ("LEVEL 1 —", "① LEADVERSE") + one-sentence explanation.
- **Real proof framed in a hand-drawn border**: screenshot of a site, Reddit post,
  research page. Characters point at it.
- **Logos as chips** next to the tools named (Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Claude).
- **One big coloured stat** ("$1,802/month", "3,711 signups") + an honesty tag
  ("SELF-REPORTED").
- **A payoff line or rating at the bottom** ("Skill level: 1/10").
- **Two short statements** rather than a paragraph (the J-Space slides).

## Rules for writing hooks from this library

1. Offer 3–5 cover options per carousel, each labelled with its pattern (H1–H8), each
   paired with the slide-2 line that pays it off.
2. Every cover gets a qualifier line (pattern from "Cover mechanics").
3. A number in the headline must match the actual slide count of the list.
4. Surprise/insider hooks (H5, H7, H8) need a real surprise in slide 2 — otherwise cut.
5. Stats like "only 2% know" need a source or get rewritten; never invented.
6. Don't copy this creator's characters or likeness; build our own recurring cast.
