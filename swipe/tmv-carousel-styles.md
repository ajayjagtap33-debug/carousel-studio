# TMV carousel styles: 30 references to reverse-engineer

For the new TMV carousel look (2026-11-02). What we publish: SEO changes, GEO tricks, citation data, studies,
AI-tool news, industry quotes. What's wrong with what the team ships now: graphs + text only, every slide the same.

Status of each reference:
- **seen**: I looked at it on Pinterest on 2026-11-02 (the login wall cut the browsing short).
- **known**: a well-known account or format I know from memory; open the account and check the latest posts
  before borrowing. Instagram is behind a login, so Ajay screenshots the good ones into `swipe/others/tmv/`.

Borrow the pattern, never the drawing, the character or the phrasing (studio rule).

## What to look for on every reference slide

1. **What leads**: one big headline, one number, one picture or one quote. If two things fight, skip the slide.
2. **Cover mechanics**: what makes the thumb stop (a face, a logo people know, a number, a "breaking" frame).
3. **Slide 2**: does it pay off the cover straight away or tease more?
4. **The recurring frame**: the 2–3 things on every slide (masthead, rule, page tab, source line) that make it
   feel like one series. This is what we turn into chrome.
5. **Real things on the slide**: screenshots, logos, faces, article clippings. Count them; good news-style
   carousels have one real thing per slide.
6. **How data is shown**: chart, single number, ranking, before/after, or a picture that stands for the number.
7. **Source line**: where and how small. Our studies need one on every data slide.
8. **Type**: how many sizes, serif or sans, where the accent colour goes.
9. **Rhythm**: how often the layout changes across 8–10 slides (dark/light flip, full-bleed photo, text-only breather).
10. **Last slide**: save/share ask, product plug or a summary card.

## A first direction (to argue about, not decided)

The current TMV slides are one family (C, charts) at one rhythm. Four families together would fix it:

- **Masthead + red news frame** (1, 29) as the series chrome: every carousel opens like a front page.
- **Real things on most slides**: screenshot with highlight (8, 18), annotated AI answer (9), tweet card (20), logos (5, 14).
- **Data that isn't a bar chart**: leaderboard with logos (14), big number (4), Wrapped blocks (15), one-claim chart (13).
- **A flip in rhythm** every 3 slides: dark bento (27) or a pull quote (21) between light slides.

New visuals this would need in `scripts/render.py` (`VIS`), built only once Ajay picks: `timeline`, `leaderboard`
(logos + bars), `tweet` (real screenshot slot with highlight), `annotate` (screenshot + marker circles/arrows),
`bigstat`, `quote`, `bento`, `myth`.
