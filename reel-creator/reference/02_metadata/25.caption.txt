Comment "JEV" to get the setup guide for Laya, the Free and Opensource Jev alternative.

Someone just released a free and open source version of Jev, and it's actually way faster and cheaper.

It's called Laya.

It's a classifier model that you can run locally on your own computer.

It does the exact same job as Jev.

But it only takes around 30 milliseconds to answer, while Jev takes around 200.

The only problem is that the base model isn't good at everything out of the box.

So it needs to be fine tuned and trained for your specific use case.

And you can do that with the help of Claude Code.

Once done, it can even match Jev on accuracy.

Think of it like getting a raw engine instead of a finished car, it's faster once you tune it, but you have to do that tuning yourself first.

Most people skip this step and end up disappointed with the base model's accuracy.

Fine tuning is really where the real performance gap closes.
