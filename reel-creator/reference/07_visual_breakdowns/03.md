## 2. Hook frame (0–2 s)
SPLIT. "Test Your Startup Idea" is black bold sans, and "On Fake Customers" is white italic serif in a blue box. The box wipes in by 0.7 s. The card is dark navy with a blue stroke: a monospace terminal-style prompt box types a question over a sketch-style crowd of people. The caption starts with "you can" and builds word by word to "your *startup idea*" (blue italic serif).

## 3. B-roll types and the lines they illustrate
- Generated prompt + crowd illustration: "test your startup idea on thousands of fake customers".
- GitHub repo + stars tile: "open source simulation engine… 80,000 stars".
- File-upload UI: "upload your product idea, pitch, or maybe a rough MVP".
- Network graph: "build an entire digital world packed with simulated AI people".
- Persona profile card: "each with their own unique personality".
- Simulated social posts: "drop them onto social media platforms and let them react, argue".
- Generated report card with colored highlight rows: "write you a full report on why they loved it, why they hated it".
- Chat UI: "chat with any one of them individually".

## 4. Captions and typography
- Bold sans word-by-word with a blue italic serif accent. On DARK the accent turns light blue ("*AI people*").
- FTH captions are white and use mixed sans plus serif italic ("you a *full report*" with the accent in blue italic).
- The editorial report card uses a bold serif headline (Georgia/Times-like) with highlighter-style colored bars behind phrases.

## 5. Graphics and overlays
- Browser cards have a blue glow border with moving light segments. There is no N/x counter (single-tool reel).
- Stars callout: a white rounded tile with a large blue "81.3k" and "stars on GitHub" in grey, which pops over the GitHub card.
- An orange ring circle highlights the topic chip "multi-agent-simulation".
- New cards start as a blank or blurred white rectangle and resolve in about 0.15–0.25 s.
