## 2. Hook frame (0–2 s)
**This is the only reel of the 17 without a title + highlight-box header.** Frame 1 is SPLIT with a large browser card (about 90 % width, y ≈ 12–43 %) already playing gameplay, and the caption starts immediately ("i just" → "i just built" → "this full" → "multiplayer" → "*attack on*"). The hook relies on the moving visual and first-person claim rather than a headline. The first FULL gameplay cut lands at 2.3 s.
