## 2. Hook frame (0–2 s)
- SPLIT. Title line 1 "Clone Any App With" is black bold sans. Line 2 "Claude Code" is white italic serif in a blue box. Words build word by word, the box wipes in, and the title is fully built by about 0.7 s.
- Card: a white rounded card with an app-icon grid (a generated graphic, not a screenshot). Icons animate in pairs: paid app → free alternative.
- At 1.7 s it hard cuts to WHITE. The title stays in place, the head panel disappears, and the Subscriptions card resolves from a blank white card. This works as a "stinger" second beat of the hook.

## 3. B-roll types and the lines they illustrate
- Generated icon grid: "clone any app".
- Generated subscription bill flipping to $0: "never have to pay for a subscription again".
- GitHub repo + rolling counter "11": "a collection of 11 skills".
- Terminal install commands: "install it in your Claude Code and point it at any app".
- App Store / G2 review screenshots with highlighted complaints: "read through the reviews… find out what users hate".
- Generated phone-screen build progress: "rebuilding it one screen at a time".
- Terminal test checklist: "adds the backend and tests every flow".
- Generated App Store listing: "give it its own branding and package it up".

## 4. Captions and typography
- Same system as 01: a bold grotesk sans (Inter/SF Pro Display Bold-like) builds word by word. New words fade in from grey. There are 1–3 words per screen.
- Accent: blue italic serif on single words ("*clone*", "*any app*", "*feature*", "*fix*", "*yours*").
- FTH emphasis: "completely" in small white serif italic over "free" in large yellow (#f5e663-ish) bold sans with glow. This mirrors the CTA style mid-video.
- Positions: SPLIT caption at y ≈ 53 %, DARK at y ≈ 72 %, FTH at y ≈ 70–75 %.

## 5. Graphics and overlays
- Browser and terminal cards have rounded corners, a thin blue glowing stroke, and moving light segments on the border.
- Counter: rolling odometer digits ("11").
- No "1/4" counter badge appears here (it is a single-tool reel). The build card instead shows "screen 3 of 18".
- In-card highlights: red highlight on complaint text, colored tag chips.
- Generated mock UIs dominate (5 of 9 B-roll beats are designed graphics rather than raw screenshots).

## 6. Background / set
- Nick in a black hoodie with a black mic. The set is the same light grey wall with a beige chair. SPLIT shows a medium shot and FTH a tight close-up.

## 7. Color notes
- Palette: #eef1f3 36 %, #403e41 20 %, #110f16 20 %, skin tones about 22 %, #53a9ee blue 1 %.
- About 50/50 light and dark. The accent is blue, and the mid-video emphasis and the CTA use yellow.

## 8. Transitions
- SPLIT → WHITE at 1.7 s: a hard cut, and the card resolves from blank.
- Into DARK shots: a hard cut, and the card fades or blurs in over a blurred background.
- FTH ↔ SPLIT: hard cuts (punch in and out).
- 29.6 s: hard cut, and the new card scales/blurs in.
- No whip or slide transitions were confirmed.
