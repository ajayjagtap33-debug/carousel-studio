DeepSeek just launched their own free version of Cloud Code, and it's already crossed 180,000 stars in GitHub in under a week.
It's called DeepSeek Harness, and unlike Cloud Code, it's fully customizable.
It's basically built in the idea that everything inside it is a plugin, including the model, the tools, the memory, and even the agent loop itself.
So you just swap out whatever modular piece you want to build your own custom AI agent entirely from scratch.
Which means you can run it with any AI model locally, and even call Cloud Code and codecs as sub-agents from inside of it.
Plus, it comes with 53 tools right out of the box, and it's completely free, as mentioned, and open source.
