## TMV (Track My Visibility)

- **Account:** the Track My Visibility brand channels, spoken by Ajay as the person behind TMV.
- **Sheet:** "TMV content machine", id `12qqX96HeeSb_KnSXDdZEjKd1LSaax1odcP7snXCrCAs`
  https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/12qqX96HeeSb_KnSXDdZEjKd1LSaax1odcP7snXCrCAs/edit
- **Topics:** SEO, AEO / GEO, search changes (Google updates, AI Overviews, AI Mode), AI visibility (being named and cited in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot), how AI assistants pick sources, content marketing that gets cited, studies and data on AI search, tools and use cases for marketers.
- **Formats:** Search update · Study / data · AI visibility how-to · Prompt check · Myth · Use case · Tool review · List · Alternative · News · Trend story · Content marketing (playbooks in formats.md; the TMV-only ones are at the end).
- **Audience:** marketers, SEOs, founders and agencies. They know "SEO"; explain AEO / GEO / AI Overviews in one plain line the first time.
- **Rules (from `~/tmv-video-plan/CLAUDE.md`):**
  - Never invent a TMV feature, click path, plan or price. Check `trackmyvisibility.com/features/` and `/pricing/`, or ask Ajay; otherwise `[CONFIRM: …]`.
  - Every number traces to a source (a study, Google's own post, TMV data Ajay gives). Third-party studies are named ("an Ahrefs study of 300k keywords…").
  - Name a competitor only to treat it fairly; invent no weakness.
  - TMV itself appears at most once, as the tool that does the step, not as the topic, unless the reel is about a TMV feature (then `tmv-script-writer` facts apply). Most TMV reels teach; the CTA sells.
  - No em dashes, no buzzwords, no rhetorical-question openers (Ajay's TMV voice rule).
- **CTA:** comment WORD for a checklist, guide, prompt list or audit (e.g. AUDIT, CHECKLIST, PROMPTS). Lead magnets that exist: the 250-point AEO & GEO checklist (keyword AUDIT). Ask Ajay before promising anything else.
- **Anchors that land:** ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews / AI Mode, Gemini, Reddit, "page one of Google", a named brand people know getting (or not getting) recommended.
